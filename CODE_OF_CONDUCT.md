# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want working on LEVIX to be predictable and safe for everyone. Contributors who
disagree will sometimes be blunt about code; that is fine. Contributors who are blunt
about *people* are not.

## Standards

Behaviours that help this project:

- Using an inclusive, direct form of address.
- Reviewing a change on its merits and stating the reasoning, not the rank.
- Accepting that a design decision has a tradeoff, and writing the tradeoff down.
- Giving people time to respond before escalating.

Behaviours that are not acceptable:

- Personal attacks, mocking, or gatekeeping based on experience level.
- Sexualised language, or attention directed at a person rather than their work.
- Publishing private information — including someone's real name, machine details or
  log output — without consent.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion, including repeating a rejected point until
  someone concedes.

## Scope

This applies inside the repository and issue tracker, in pull requests, and in any
public representation of the project. A maintainer reviewing your patch is also
covered.

## Reporting

Report to the maintainer privately via a
[security advisory](https://github.com/shivamsinghtomar78/LEVIX/security/advisories/new)
or the contact listed in `README.md`. Reports are handled as confidentially as
vulnerability reports. The person reporting gets a plain statement of what happened,
and the person reported gets the same chance to respond before any action is taken.

## Enforcement

Consequences scale with the harm and the pattern:

1. Private written notice, with the specific behaviour quoted.
2. Public notice and a temporary ban from the affected space.
3. Permanent ban from all project spaces.

Decisions can be revisited with new information. Maintainers who apply no consequence
to their own behaviour can be reported under the same process.
